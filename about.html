Welcome!

The wifiskeleton scaredy cat (often referred to as the yellow scaredy cat) is an iconic mascot character associated with the late musician and artist Wifiskeleton (Jeremiah Simms). Characterized by its bright yellow body, wide startled eyes, yellow fur, and nervously chaotic expression, the yellow cat became a recurring motif across Wifiskeleton's music cover art, album previews, and social media visuals. The character frequently appears in albums perfectly capturing the dark, humorous, and aesthetic of Wifiskeleton's music.
